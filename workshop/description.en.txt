Reached Europa, but still chatting one line of text at a time?
Want to send a reaction image instead of making your crewmates imagine it?

[h2]Just typing messages... getting boring, isn't it?[/h2]
Bring your own emotes aboard—with images and GIFs in chat and above your character!

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Import your own PNG, JPG and GIF files. Send still images or animated reactions.
[*]Press F8 for a thumbnail picker, then click to send. Local names and transfer progress are supported.
[*]Emotes appear in chat history. Normal/radio messages also show a bubble above the speaker for about six seconds; private and dead chat do not create overhead bubbles.
[*]Missing assets sync automatically to eligible recipients. Cached sends reuse the asset, so everyone does not need the same image library beforehand.
[*]Uses your current chat channel and native distance, radio, private and dead-chat rules. Emote reception and animation can be switched off.
[/list]

[h2]How to use[/h2]
1. Requires Barotrauma 1.13.5.0, LuaCs and C# support on the host/dedicated server and clients.
2. Double-click EmoteImporter.exe in the mod's Tools folder to select images, or drag PNG/JPG/GIF files directly onto that EXE. Source users can run the Python importer.
3. Join the server, press F8, and click an emote. Reopen the picker after importing new files.
4. The first transfer shows progress; later sends reuse the cache. The bubble timer starts when the asset is ready.

[h2]Safety and performance[/h2]
[list]
[*]Original images are processed only in the separate local importer. Multiplayer sends normalized pixel animations, without source files, local paths or file names.
[*]The game never decodes remote GIF/PNG/JPG files, executes emote code, or fetches image URLs. The server uses the real sender connection and checks recipient permissions and SHA-256 integrity.
[*]File size, dimensions, frame count, duration and decompressed size are bounded. Malformed/oversized assets are rejected. Transfers are rate-limited and expire; caches have fixed budgets.
[*]Validation/decoding runs in background work. Assets share a texture and refresh only when visible and the frame changes, with at most two texture uploads per update—no per-frame disk reads or GIF decoding.
[/list]
Long animations keep their first ten seconds; large/high-frame-rate animations are resized or sampled. Your original files stay local.
